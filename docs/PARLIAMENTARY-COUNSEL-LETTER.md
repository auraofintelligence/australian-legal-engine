# Asking for access, rather than taking it

The engine reads documents you already hold and makes no network calls.
For a personal read-through that is enough: you download the acts that
touch your life from the register, the same as anyone.

Going further, to a standing index of law that stays current, needs
something the engine cannot give itself: sanctioned bulk access from the
office that publishes each jurisdiction's legislation. The registers set
terms on automated access and Crown copyright applies to the reprints, so
the way through is to ask.

Below is a template. It is deliberately plain, names the project, and
makes the non-commercial position clear up front, because that is usually
the first question.

## Who to write to

Current contact details are published on each register, linked from
https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/pages/nine-registers.html

## Template

> Subject: Request for bulk access to [jurisdiction] legislation for a
> non-commercial public reading tool
>
> Dear [office],
>
> I am writing to ask what options exist for bulk or programmatic access
> to [jurisdiction] legislation, and under what conditions.
>
> I maintain a free, non-commercial tool that helps people read the laws
> that apply to their own lives. It parses acts into their sections and
> subsections, searches them offline on the reader's own computer, and
> answers a question by quoting the provision and citing it, so the reader
> can check the wording against your register. It gives legal information
> only, never legal advice, and it says so on every answer.
>
> The tool currently reads only documents a person has already downloaded
> themselves. I would rather ask about sustainable access than build
> around your terms of use, which is why I am writing.
>
> Specifically, I would like to know:
>
> 1. Whether bulk downloads or a data feed of in-force legislation are
>    available, in any format, and on what conditions.
> 2. What your office's position is on automated retrieval from the
>    register where no bulk option exists.
> 3. What attribution and currency wording you would want shown beside
>    any provision displayed from your data.
> 4. Whether there is a licence, agreement or notification process I
>    should complete first.
>
> The project is published openly at
> https://github.com/auraofintelligence/australian-legal-engine
> and the wider method it belongs to at
> https://auraofintelligence.github.io/australian-law-2012-lukes-relevance/
>
> It is licensed for non-commercial use, and I am not seeking to
> republish your material as a commercial product or to present it as an
> authorised version.
>
> Thank you for your time.
>
> [name, contact details]

## Notes on sending

- One office at a time. The answers will differ, and a reply from one is
  not permission from another.
- Keep every reply. What each office says, and the date it said it,
  belongs beside the data it governs.
- A "no" is still useful: it tells you that jurisdiction stays
  download-by-hand, and the engine already works that way.
